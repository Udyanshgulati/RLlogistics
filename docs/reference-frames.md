# Reference frames — what each one shows

Frames come from a screen recording of the reference site, in time order. We copy the **motion language and story structure**, not the content or branding.

| # | Time | What's on screen | Motion / technique to borrow |
|---|------|------------------|------------------------------|
| 01 | 1.3s | Dark hero. Vertical tiny-caps nav list on the left, eyebrow "ONE OPERATOR", giant 3-line wide display headline, short sub-copy. Right half: a big **dotted-land globe** cropped by the viewport, orange/red fresnel rim top-left, electric-blue rim bottom-right. Labelled pins with leader lines, one pulsing red. Pill CTA top right. | Load-in reveal; globe idle rotation; pulsing pin; labels with leader lines. |
| 02 | 2.3s | We've dived through the globe: full-screen **blue → white atmosphere gradient**. A landscape image card on the left, a paragraph on the right shown as two misregistered copies, headline clipped bottom-left. | Globe dive → atmosphere gradient → paper. Ghost/double-exposure text settling. |
| 03 | 2.7s | Light paper section. Headline "WE MOVE FREIGHT / WE OWN…" with grey (unrevealed) vs black (revealed) lines. Right column: big stat "2 500+" rendered as a doubled/offset image converging, label under it doubled too. | Counter with vertical blur/odometer; ghost copies converging; scrubbed grey→ink reveal. |
| 04 | 3.0s | Stats continue ("98.2%", "8+ Years in operation"), headline fully revealed ink. A machine starts entering bottom-left. | Stats appear as you scroll; next scene's object overlaps the exit. |
| 05 | 3.7s | White yard: side-view **reach-stacker** (body, telescopic boom, spreader) with its spreader on a white container stacked on navy + orange containers. | Layered vehicle illustration, boom rotates on pivot. |
| 06 | 4.3s | Container lifted off the stack, motion-blurred/double image, scene zoomed in. | Camera zoom on the scene; lift + rotate; ghosting during fast moves. |
| 07 | 5.0s | Camera close on the container lowered onto a **truck flatbed trailer**, spreader still attached. | Container lands on trailer; truck enters from the right. |
| 08 | 5.4s | Zoomed back out: loaded truck sits on the top edge of a **dark panel rising from below** (panel acts as the road). Giant faint text peeks in at right. | clip-path panel rise; truck parked on the panel edge. |
| 09 | 6.0s | Dark services section: service columns (icon, title, copy) each shown as **doubled ghost copies** sliding into register; paragraphs left. Truck still on top edge. | Ghost stagger for icons + titles; watermark text. |
| 10 | 6.7s | Dark panel taking over, edges curving — the start of the road. | Transition into top-down road. |
| 11 | 7.0s | Top-down **black road** with a rounded corner turning into a vertical strip, white lane dash. Headline "RELIABILITY / AT EVERY / MILESTONE" ghosting in on the left. | Road path scale/straighten; ghost heading. |
| 12 | 7.4s | Road is now a vertical strip in the centre of a white page; a top-down truck drives down it. Feature items (icon, title, copy) appear on the right as the truck passes. | Truck on vertical road; milestones triggered by truck position. |
| 13 | 8.2s | Full-bleed deep-blue sea, **top-down container ship** extremely zoomed in — colourful container rows fill the frame, white foam along hull. | Start zoomed in on ship. |
| 14 | 9.0s | Zooming out, ship travelling up; white heading "LOGISTICS THAT WORKS…" ghosting in over the ship. | Scrubbed zoom-out; ghost heading in white. |
| 15 | 9.3s | Ship smaller; heading leaving top. Feature blocks either side ("ONE POINT OF CONTACT", "FULL SUPPLY CHAIN VISIBILITY") with icons, ghost copies. | Features around the ship. |
| 16 | 9.7s | Ship small and centred; another feature block centred above it. | Continuing zoom-out. |
| 17 | 10.1s | Ship tiny; **clouds** rolling in from the edges over the blue. | Layered cloud planes with parallax. |
| 18 | 10.4s | A white **top-down plane** fills the frame passing across; behind it the paper section with "TRUSTED BY BU…" is revealed. | Plane wipes clouds → paper. |
| 19 | 11.0s | Testimonials: heading "TRUSTED / BY BUSINESSES / ACROSS …" (last line grey), photo card + name, long quote doubled/ghosted resolving, plane tail at the right edge. | Testimonial reveal; plane tail as decoration. |

**Recurring language:** wide heavy all-caps display type; tiny UI labels; ghost / double-exposure text that settles into register; grey → ink scrubbed reveals; every scene shares an object or colour with the next so the scroll reads as one camera move.
